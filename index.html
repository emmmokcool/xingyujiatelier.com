Made to Order

Choose silk in person, be measured in the bra you plan to wear with your Qipao, and lightweight clothes. Receive a new one-of-a-kind design made for you — not a copy of a sold piece.

01

Explore

Review the process and starting fees before reaching out.

02

Meet

A 90-minute weekday evening appointment in Bellevue.

03

Confirm

Select fabric and direction, pay the production deposit, then approve the work order.

04

Collect

Typical production is 4–6 weeks, followed by a local final try-on and pickup.

Made-to-order Qipao starts at $650 before applicable tax.

Exquisite silk is reserved for established clients only.
