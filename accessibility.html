Accessibility

Last updated 21 August 2026

This studio was founded on the belief that clothing should adapt to the person, not the other way around. The same principle applies to how we build this site and run appointments.

On this site

  • A skip link is provided to jump straight to the main content.
  • Navigation, filters, and dialogs are operable by keyboard.
  • Images carry descriptive alternative text, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and background colors are chosen for contrast, and the layout reflows down to 280px wide.
  • Content is structured with real headings and landmarks so it can be navigated by assistive technology.

In the studio

  • Fittings are private and one-to-one, at a pace you set.
  • Measurements are taken in the clothing you are comfortable wearing. You may bring a companion.
  • Garments are designed to honor varied bodies; sizing is built from your measurements rather than a standard size chart.
  • Tell us in advance about mobility, sensory, or scheduling needs and we will adapt the appointment.

Known limitations

This is a small, actively developed site built by a one-person studio. Some interactive elements are still being refined, and a live checkout is planned for a future release. If anything blocks you, we want to hear about it rather than have you work around it.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier on this site or need information in another format, email sales@xingyujiatelier.com. Describe the page and what happened, and we will respond and correct it as quickly as we can.
